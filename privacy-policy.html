Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Razlax Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") manages, collects, and protects user data across all mobile applications published by us on the Google Play Store (including Pizza Rewards and associated utility/reward applications).

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure account management, fair reward point distribution, and smooth app experiences, our applications may collect and process:

  • Account Identifiers: Basic user ID, email address, or device identifiers used to verify reward ledgers and user streak completions.
  • Device & Network Data: Device model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type, and Google Advertising ID (GAID) for fraud prevention, analytics, and diagnostics.
  • In-App Activity: Daily streak progress, offerwall interaction status, and redeemed coin history.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising Partners

Our applications integrate trusted industry-standard SDKs for backend management, authentication, analytics, and advertising mediation:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Account authentication, real-time database syncing, and crash diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob & Mediation: Delivery of banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads across mediated advertising partners.
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook): High-yield programmatic advertising and contextual ad delivery integrated via AdMob Mediation. Ad serving and attribution adhere to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • PubScale (Offerwall): Offerwall integration, playtime engagement tracking, and cost-per-action reward delivery verified via secure Server-to-Server (S2S) callbacks.

3. Advertising & Data Usage

Ad networks integrated within our apps (such as Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network) may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as GAID) and app interaction data to deliver contextual, relevant ads and measure ad performance in compliance with applicable platform policies.

4. Anti-Fraud & Fair Play Security

We implement strict automated safeguards against unauthorized multi-accounting, emulator exploitation, proxy/VPN manipulation, and automated scripts. Data transmission is secured using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been collected mistakenly, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
