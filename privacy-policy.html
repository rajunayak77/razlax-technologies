Privacy Policy for Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy specifically governs the mobile application Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code (Package: com.cricky.rewards), developed, published, and maintained by Razlax Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and rights of our users.

1. Information We Access, Collect, and Process

In full compliance with Google Play User Data policies, our application collects only the necessary information to maintain reward balance integrity, prevent fraud, and facilitate legitimate reward redemptions:

  • Personal Information: User ID and email address (provided during login or authentication) used to uniquely identify user profiles and sync game streak data securely via Google Firebase.
  • Financial Information: User-submitted Virtual Payment Address (UPI ID) or registered payout handles collected exclusively when a user voluntarily initiates a withdrawal request for earned digital rewards. We do not store credit/debit card numbers or net banking credentials.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Google Advertising ID (GAID), operating system version, device model, and network carrier status for fraud prevention, diagnostics, and anti-abuse enforcement.
  • In-App Actions & Progress: Daily check-in streaks, trivia mini-game points, and offerwall completion states.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising Mediation

To enable real-time cloud data storage, fraud mitigation, rewarded offerwalls, and monetization, Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code integrates the following industry-certified SDKs:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Authentication, cloud database synchronization, and crash reporting analytics.
  • Google AdMob: Programmatic banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad serving adhering to Google AdMob Policies.
  • Meta Audience Network: Contextual and personalized in-app advertising delivered via AdMob Mediation bidding. Read the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • PubScale (Offerwall): Rewarded gameplay and third-party sponsored tasks credited via secure Server-to-Server (S2S) postbacks.

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill reward requests, and maintain audit records:

  • Data Retention: Payout transaction records are retained for auditing purposes. Inactive accounts may be purged periodically.
  • User Deletion Rights: You have the legal right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. Simply send an email with your registered email ID or User ID to support@razlaxtechnologies.in with the subject "Delete My Data". All associated personal records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

4. Security Safeguards & Anti-Fraud Protection

We utilize industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption for all data sent between the app and our secure servers. Any attempt to bypass game mechanics using auto-clickers, VPN/proxy spoofing, rooted devices, or emulators will lead to immediate account suspension in accordance with our terms.

5. Children's Privacy

Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it promptly.
