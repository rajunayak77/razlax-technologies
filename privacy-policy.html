Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Razlax Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects information across all mobile applications published on the Google Play Store (including Pizza Rewards and associated utility/reward apps).

1. Information We Collect

To ensure smooth app performance, secure session management, and verified reward payouts, our applications may collect:

  • Account Identifiers: Basic user ID, email address, or device ID used to track reward ledgers and streak progress.
  • Device Information: Google Advertising ID (GAID), device brand/model, OS version, and network state for fraud prevention and diagnostics.
  • In-App Activity: Daily streak completions, offer interactions, and game points.

2. Third-Party Integrations

We work with trusted industry SDKs to provide backend database synchronization and monetization:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Authentication, realtime database syncing, and crash analytics.
  • Google AdMob & Google AdX: Contextual banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad inventory.
  • PubScale (Offerwall): Playtime and CPI offerwalls with Server-to-Server (S2S) callback verification.

3. Security & Fraud Protection

We strictly protect user data through encrypted HTTPS/TLS communication protocols and secure cloud rules. Automated anti-fraud algorithms monitor unauthorized tampering, multi-accounting, and proxy/VPN abuse.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, contact our support immediately for deletion.
