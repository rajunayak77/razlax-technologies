Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code (com.cricky.rewards)

Developer: Razlax Technologies

In compliance with Google Play User Data policies, users of Pizza Rewards - Redeem Code have full rights to request the deletion of their app account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion at any time without needing to reinstall the app. Please follow either of these methods:

Method 1: Direct Email Request (Fastest)

Send an email from your registered Google Play account email address to our support team:

Email Address: support@razlaxtechnologies.in

Subject Line: Delete My Pizza Rewards Account

Include in Email: Your registered email ID and in-app User ID / Refer Code (if available).

Request Account Deletion via Email

Method 2: In-App Deletion

Open the Pizza Rewards app > Go to Profile / Settings > Tap on Delete Account > Confirm deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your deletion request is verified and processed, the following data is permanently purged within 7 business days:

  • Registered account credentials (Name, Email ID, Profile picture URL).
  • Reward coin balance, points, and redemption transaction records.
  • Advertising identifiers (GAID) and device-specific tokens stored in our database.

Data Retention Policy

Certain minimal transactional records (such as processed UPI/Redemption logs) may be retained for up to 90 days solely to comply with legal, statutory, and fraud-prevention requirements, after which they are permanently wiped.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this deletion policy, please write to us at support@razlaxtechnologies.in.
